## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a luxury commercial furniture and workspace storage e-commerce presence tailored for the East African corporate market. Rooted in an aesthetic fusion of warm organic minimalism and Scandinavian executive discipline, the experience balances functional utility with architectural prestige.

The target audience comprises enterprise facilities managers, corporate interior architects, studio founders, and executive decision-makers in Kenya and across East Africa seeking enduring, high-grade workspace pieces. The UI evokes confidence, quiet authority, craftsmanship, and grounded warmth. 

The aesthetic marries:
- **Warm Minimalist Architecture:** Generous ivory and alabaster canvases, structured negative space, and disciplined layout rhythm instead of visual clutter.
- **Prestige Materiality:** Polished gold accents, deep charcoal typography, subtle parchment-toned borders, and textural imagery of fine joinery, treated acacia, leather, and powder-coated steel.
- **Editorial Commerce:** Crisp categorization, curated showroom-like displays, clear B2B and direct pricing transparency in Kenyan Shillings (KSh), and tactile interactions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, luminous warmth inspired by the golden acacia landscape and architectural light.

- **Primary Accent (`#E2A028`):** Acacia Gold. Used for hero CTAs, key status indicators, active tabs, prestige badges, and curated price highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B87B15`):** Warm Amber. Deployed for interactive hover and pressed states, focused focus rings, and high-contrast gold tier surfaces.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#292524`):** Warm Slate Onyx. Serves as an auxiliary dark surface for secondary action buttons, footer backgrounds, and dark contrast banners.
- **Neutral Core (`#1C1917`):** Deep Charcoal Onyx. The primary typographic anchor for high-legibility headings, key specifications, and critical icons.

### Surface Tokens
- **Background Canvas:** `#FAF9F5` (Warm Alabaster). Provides a calm, low-glare surface across all viewport tiers.
- **Container / Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White). Separates interactive products and specs from the backdrop.
- **Subtle Border Line:** `#E5E2DA` (Parchment Neutral). Delineates product cards, dividers, filters, and header rules with subtle elegance.
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#78716C` (Stone Gray). Used for dimension metadata, breadcrumbs, SKU numbers, and delivery timeframes.
- **Feedback & Availability:** `#2E7D32` (In Stock Forest Green), `#C62828` (Low Stock Brick).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Montserrat** for geometric, confident structural headlines and commercial price figures, paired with **Inter** for neutral, utilitarian specifications, dimensions, and long-form body copy.

- **Headlines & Editorial Hooks:** Montserrat is rendered in Medium and Semi-Bold weights with tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` on titles above 32px) to reflect modern architectural discipline.
- **Body & Product Attributes:** Inter provides clean, rhythmically sound reading for material compositions, technical specifications, and logistics details.
- **Price Hierarchy:** Pricing must always use Montserrat with bold or semi-bold weight. Currency prefixes ("KSh") must be formatted in a slightly smaller size or medium weight directly preceding the integer (e.g., `KSh 148,000`).
- **Uppercase Labels:** Category eyebrow tags, micro badges, and navigational tabs utilize Montserrat in uppercase with `0.08em` tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of 1440px for desktop screens.

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Catalog grids support standard 3-column and 4-column product matrices.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 32px page margins. Catalog surfaces collapse into a 2-column or 3-column flow.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 20px (`1.25rem`) page margins. Product listings feature either a high-impact single-column stacked display or a tight 2-column card view with quick-add functionality.

All section-level vertical rhythms adhere to an 8px modular baseline, employing generous `space-xl` gaps to grant commercial furniture imagery breathable architectural framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes Scandinavian clarity over heavy dropped shadows. Depth is communicated primarily through crisp tonal layering, subtle neutral borders, and warm ambient diffusion.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF9F5` base background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px solid stroke of `#E5E2DA`. No harsh shadow; an ultra-soft ambient bloom `0 2px 10px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)` provides separation.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product Cards):** Elevates slightly with `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`, lifting the product presentation toward the viewer and subtly darkening the perimeter border to `#D6D1C7`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Executive Navbars & Filter Drawers):** Backdrop blur of `16px` combined with 90% white opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`) and a bottom hairline divider `#E5E2DA`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Quote Inquiries):** Deep backdrop mask of `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.45)` with ambient modal shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural edge profile calibrated to **Level 1 (Soft)**.

- **Standard Buttons, Cards, & Inputs:** Border-radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`). This provides crisp, tailor-made corporate precision while avoiding sterile harshness.
- **Pills & Status Badges:** Specific circular chips (such as stock indicators or category pill filters) utilize fully rounded ends (`9999px`) to visually contrast against square-edged architectural product photography.
- **Product Image Vessels:** 4px radius applied to image masks with subtle neutral perimeter bounds to ensure consistent display across varying furniture finishes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Acacia Gold (`#E2A028`) fill with Deep Charcoal Onyx (`#1C1917`) text, 4px radius, medium bold typography. On hover, transitions to Warm Amber (`#B87B15`) with text shifting to pure white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Pure white or transparent background with a 1.5px border in Charcoal Onyx (`#1C1917`), darkening to full `#1C1917` fill with white text on hover.
- **Ghost / Link Buttons:** Deep Charcoal text with an underlined gold bar appearing on hover accompanied by a forward arrow icon.

### Product Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with a 1px `#E5E2DA` border and 4px radius.
- Contains an aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1 image frame with an off-white photo backdrop.
- Features top metadata: Category eyebrow (e.g., "EXECUTIVE STORAGE"), product name in Montserrat Semi-Bold, dimensions indicator, and bold KSh pricing.
- Quick-action wishlist icon in the upper-right corner and an anchored "Configure / Add to Cart" button revealed or highlighted on hover.

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Curated / New Arrival Badge:** Pill-shaped, warm gold tinted background (`#FFF8EC`) with Acacia Gold text (`#B87B15`) and a subtle border.
- **Material Chips:** Compact 24x24px round swatches (Natural Acacia, Smoked Oak, Matte Black Steel, Desert Stone) with an active focus ring of Acacia Gold.

### Input Fields & Select Dropdowns
- 44px minimum touch target, white background, 1px `#E5E2DA` border, 4px radius.
- Focus state activates a 1.5px border in Acacia Gold (`#E2A028`) with zero heavy outer glow, preserving architectural crispness.
- Placeholder text in `#78716C`.

### Corporate B2B Quote & Spec Drawer
- Dedicated sliding drawer for commercial volume pricing, bulk CAD model downloads, and delivery logistics within Nairobi and nationwide Kenya. Structured with tonal dividers and clear table-like layout hierarchy.
